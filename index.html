CALLED IT!

How well do you know what changes someone's mind?

Pass-and-play

Choose a Judge. The Judge reads the scenario and options aloud. Everyone, including the Judge, secretly writes their first answer. Read the twist aloud, then everyone secretly writes their second answer. All guessers reveal and explain their predictions first. Finally, the Judge reveals both actual choices.

Score: 0 points if neither prediction is right, 1 point if only one is right, or 3 points if both are right. The Judge scores 0 points.

THE TWIST

Judge: secretly write your second choice (you may keep your first). Everyone else: secretly write your second prediction. Then all guessers reveal BOTH predictions and explain why. Only after that does the Judge reveal both actual choices. Score 0, 1, or 3 points.
