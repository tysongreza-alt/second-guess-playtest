SECOND GUESS

How well do you know what changes someone's mind?

Pass-and-play

Choose a Spotlight player. Read the question and let everyone write their prediction. The Spotlight player records their original choice secretly. Reveal the twist, then everyone predicts their final choice.

Score: 1 point for the correct first prediction; 2 points for the correct final prediction. The Spotlight player earns 1 point for taking their turn.

THE TWIST

Spotlight: secretly decide whether to keep your choice or change it. Everyone else: write down your final prediction. Then reveal and score.
